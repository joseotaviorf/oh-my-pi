## Metric Growth Demand

### Purpose

This DAG creates daily, weekly, and monthly metrics following the new taxonomy for growth performance marketing.

### Execution Interval

Daily. More information about run time [here]({chart_url}{dag_id}).

### Outputs

Currently, this DAG creates the following output tables in the metric layer:

- `metric_growth.growth_demand_performance_daily`
- `metric_growth.growth_demand_performance_weekly`
- `metric_growth.growth_demand_performance_monthly`
