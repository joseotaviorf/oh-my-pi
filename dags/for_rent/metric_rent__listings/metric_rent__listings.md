## Metric Rent Listings

### Purpose

The listings domain follows the house publication, and intermediate steps until its rent. 
Here, it usually is interesting to follow conversions during the listing life.

### Execution Interval

Daily. More information about run time [here]({chart_url}{dag_id}).
