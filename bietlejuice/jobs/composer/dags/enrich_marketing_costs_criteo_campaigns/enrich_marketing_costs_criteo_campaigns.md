## Enrich Marketing Costs Criteo Campaigns

### Purpose

Marketing Costs Criteo Campaigns gathers information about Crite Campaigns media costs performed by marketing team. The enrich process simply creates an ID column as composite key.

### Execution Interval

Daily. More information about run time [here]({chart_url}{dag_id}).

### Outputs

Currently, there are the following output tables in our enrich layer:

- `criteo_campaigns`
