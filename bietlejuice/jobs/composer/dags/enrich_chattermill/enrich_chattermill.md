## Composer

### Purpose

Prepares Chattermill information for business utilization. Chattermill is an external service that does unified customer feedback analytics. In our case, we mostly use it to process NPS data.

### Execution Interval

Daily. More information about run time [here]({chart_url}{dag_id}).

### Outputs

We load the _Responses_ table, that gets NPS survey analytics informations.
