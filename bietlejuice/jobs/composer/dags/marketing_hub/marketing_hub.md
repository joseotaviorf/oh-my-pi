## Marketing Hub

### Purpose

Marketing Hub brings our marketing campaigns data from Google platform. It uses [ads-performance](https://github.com/quintoandar/ads-performance) developed by Marketing Tools to reach the API.

### Execution Interval

Daily. More information about run time [here]({chart_url}{dag_id}).

### Outputs

Currently, there is the following output table for both our raw and clean layers:

- `google_ads_performance_report`
- `google_keywords_performance_report`
- `google_campaigns_performance_report`
- `google_videos_performance_report`
