## Enrich EBDB Customer Contact Identification

### Purpose

Creates enriched tables for the context `Customer Contact Identification` of ebdb 
adding information like emails and phone numbers for customer.

### Execution Interval

Daily. More information about run time [here]({chart_url}{dag_id}).

### Outputs

Produces the following output tables:
