## GSHEETS
​
### Purpose
​
This dag extracts data from Google Sheets files and put into datalake raw.
​
### Dag Dependencies
None

### Dependent Dags
None

### Execution​ Interval
This dag is trigged once per day 1 AM BRT.

### Outputs
​
This pipeline produces all tables defined in gsheets_files.yaml
